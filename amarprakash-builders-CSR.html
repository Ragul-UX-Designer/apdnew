Amarprakash Foundations

Our Society, Our Responsibility

We do not just build structures, but we care much more than that. Yes, "Community first" is our operational motto at Amarprakash and we pride ourselves for bringing up Amarprakash Foundations for this society. All our CSR activities we are running are under the name of Amarprakash Foundations. We understand it is mandatory for us to return good that the community has offered us. By Amarprakash Foundations, we understand the need of behaving responsible in our society not just to benefit the community but also to benefit ourselves.

HEALTH AND SAFETY

We commit towards a process of comprehensible and continuous improvement in pursuit of health and safety excellence. Robust systems and controls enable us to manage our health and safety activity to achieve zero accidents. Besides this, we have organized many health camps from general to specialist examiners to serve the humanity. In addition, we have executed a successful field guide for detection, management and surveillance of impurities in potable water, since water is the primary source of malicious infections and because it is the vital source of life.

COMMUNITY INVOLVEMENT

Construction organizations play a vital role while develop local communities through sustained investment and development in the built environment. We recognize how important our responsibilities are in carrying out this role and we continue to contribute to the society development by assisting the backward students, through fees, books and free health check-up camps irrespective of caste or creed. On a larger scale, the company is providing jobs to over 2000 & above, thus giving a chance to individuals to live with dignity. Affirming a tangible commitment to social responsibility and ethical citizenship in the community. We encourage our people to involve themselves in the local communities where they work, either through volunteer activities in local organizations and charities or by taking part in local business groups and educational establishments in sharing knowledge and experience.
