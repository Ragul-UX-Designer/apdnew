Apartments in Pallavaram

One of the oldest residential localities in South Chennai, Pallavaram has long since been in the realty radar of development and growth. Considered to be an ideal location for domestic investment, the area is located at the center to most other well developed locations. The number of Pallavaram apartments for sale has doubled over the years, and the infrastructure growth in the area is stupendous. Located 17 kms from the center of Chennai, the area offers good connectivity to Thoraipakkam and Shollinganallur. This acts as a major booster for the popularity of Pallavaram. Owing to a rising housing demand, the property prices of apartments in Pallavaram have shot up in recent times. Most prospective buyers consider the location of the property to be its main USP. Pallavaram gains on both residential and commercial aspects of property.

Connectivity and accessibility are high points in Pallavaram apartments for sale

Property developers are gaining immense interest in the suburbs of South Chennai. The apartments are available in both luxury and affordable housing segments. The social and economic infrastructure in the locality has progressed significantly over the years impacting the realty industry too. The Thoraipakkam-Pallavaram corridor that connects the IT hub sector to the suburb is now a major commuting road for the residents living at apartments in Pallavaram. The advantage of easy accessibility to workplaces nearby, presence of educational institutes, hospitals, and easy access to shopping areas has helped the residents of Pallavaram to live comfortably. Among several apartments for sale in Pallavaram Chennai, the newly launched ones are gaining more momentum over the past few months. As the appreciation and return values of the area are high, it finds a good place among prospective investors.

With a distance of 4kms from the Chennai airport, apartments in Pallavaram Chennai are a popular destination among frequent travellers. Even those, who have moved new into the city, are sure to find an accommodation here. When it comes to rental values, reasonable rental trends are viewed across the locality. Rents are based on the buying and selling rates that keep changing every quarter based on the realty sector business. The high return investments with Pallavaram apartments for sale have also catapulted several developers into developing new areas. Once a project is introduced, the area surrounding it also flourishes at a fast pace.

Good civic and social infrastructure has also helped to create a new identity for the Pallavaram locality. While some of the apartments in Pallavaram are regular 1 BHK, there are also many 2 and 3 BHK homes available at reasonable rates. The realty scenario in Chennai is emerging on a positive note and several developers are expected to plan their projects with wide variety of choices in apartments for sale in Pallavaram Chennai. The appreciation of the area has increased over the years with several high end homes being constructed too. Luxury apartments that exude classy living and many amenities are present within the project complex. The apartments in Pallavaram Chennai are profitable for the buyers and have high rental values too.

The nearby IT parks and manufacturing units attract a lot of workforce that has enabled housing demand to increase over the years. The concept of township living has also blossomed and several investors are buying apartments in Pallavaram for the community living idea. This trend is likely to gain prominence in the coming years.
