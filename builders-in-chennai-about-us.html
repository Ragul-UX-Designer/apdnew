Who We Are

Amarprakash Developers Private Limited is a premium residential construction firm founded in 2004. Since its beginning, Amarprakash has set the highest standards for itself. It has made a noteworthy presence in modern dimensions of residential real estate development. Today, Amarprakash has grown into one of the most awarded real estate companies in Chennai acclaimed for its outstanding residential projects with State-Of-The-Art Amenities. Amarprakash follows elegant style in construction that assist modern architectural philosophy and Vastu aspects. The journey over 15 years in the industry has gained us the name of 'Tamilnadu's Most Trusted Developer'.
