Chennai Residential Projects are Witnessing Growth

Home Residential Projects
Residential Projects

In India, Chennai ranks 4th in the list of popular cities with its numerous developments in industrial sectors such as healthcare industries, automobile, hardware, information technology etc. The amalgamation of government policies, infrastructure betterments and other encouraging factors have transformed into the growth of property market in the city. Due to rampant expansion of realty market, many new residential projects in Chennai were launched in recent years to successfully satisfy the housing needs.

The recent case study about the Chennai property market revealed that residential assets were under pressure over the past couple of years but now positive signs are evolving around the properties. In the present day, we could see launch of many Chennai residential projects with its demand in the property sector. Here comes the good time for the common buyers who were waiting over the fence for prolonged period to buy an asset since the price of properties were stable now. Henceforth, the price graph of housing projects in Chennai is predicted to be stable for a period of 10 to 12 months in the coming year. So, the experts anticipate a highest number of property transactions in the upcoming period.

New Residential Projects in Chennai Suburbs:

Today, real estate builders are concentrating much on the suburbs of the metro for constructing housing units. The reason behind this is suburbs are noticing maximum value appreciation of up to 30%, so an increased number of ongoing residential projects in Chennai suburbs were recorded. Within the stretch, south city is witnessing an unmatched expansion in the asset segment. The changing banking and financial institutions, entry of foreign retailers, expanding IT/ ITEs hubs and other service segments are some key factors contributed to the growth of rising rates of residential projects in Chennai suburbs.

With the demand for housing units climbing higher by each day, the prices of assets in emerging localities like Chrompet, OMR, Pallikaranai, Velachery, Perungudi, Sholinganallur, Kelambakkam, Medavakkam etc are going to increase triple times in another 2 years. Moreover, the buyers show no hesitation to own an asset in Chennai residential projects with the presence of full-fledged social developments like schools, shopping arcade, entertainment regions, hospitals and lots more in these regions. Furthermore, over-supply of properties here is also due to huge vacancy of land parcels and easy accessibility. Additionally, owning an abode in the new residential projects in Chennai is an ideal choice for those who want to ride on affordability aspect since property units are available within the pocket of different walks of income group.

People who want to live near to their work station will start moving into the upcoming residential projects in Chennai to be without compromising on the traveling time. These areas also remained on the chart of top preferred locations in the city for rental accommodations. Vicinity to IT hubs is the popular reason for people to make their settlement in rental abodes within housing projects in Chennai. Hence, the owners will be offered with good rental returns on monthly basis because the houses positioned here are in greater demand among renters.

So moving forth, the home aspirers can move forward towards the transaction of homes in the residential projects in Chennai suburbs due to the strengthening of property market. Moreover, this is the right time for the investors to invest in these places to gain higher capital appreciation over the months because of the healthy demand for assets. Also, at present the cost of properties in Chennai residential projects remains stable and strong.
