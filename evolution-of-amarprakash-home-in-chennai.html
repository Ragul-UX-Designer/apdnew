Evolution of Amarprakash Developers Pvt Ltd

Since 1950

The history of AMARPRAKASH goes back to 1950. While Shri. Amarchand Surana involved in creating innovative business, products and services including finance and real estate investments. Further, he took their first step towards the electrical manufacturing under the name "Allied Industries" in 1970 believing electrical would be the wave of future. The products that they produced proved popular, earning the company the reputation for the high quality at affordable prices.

From 1980

The next generation entrepreneur our Chairman, Mr. Sudhir Kumar Surana, a prominent member of the community, a religion and social activist, continued the legacy and setup a new business in the year of 1980 into automobile sector, which was new to Chennai. He diversified his interest into various sectors of finance business like an automobile, real estate, personal loan etc., and hence worked round the clock to keep on progress schedule. Dissatisfied by the lack of challenge and his eagerness to contribute to create the society and improve people's lives, in 2004 he expanded his interest from finance to real estate, infrastructure and property development, and set his sights on construction of quality homes at an affordable price range for average earners. Because, owning a home is a hard task, and were expensive for most of the people.

The Next Generation

Our Managing Director, Mr. Aadarsh Kumar Surana got inspire from his forefathers, with his father's words of caution and encouragement about the life he has to face in this real estate industry, he took the company "Amarprakash Developers" to a higher altitude, undaunted by the meagre resources with just a few members recruited through word of mouth. With less than 10 years, at present, the company has evolved and developed in to one of the top real estate companies in Tamilnadu with a corporate office head quartered in Chennai.

Today

All these facts engineered the ideology of our managing director is the willingness to offer better living solutions to the people with a sense of security, comfort and joy. Further, the basic management aim of the company is to devote to work has resulted in innovative and superior products. The first project launch was well below the price tags of competitors and soon it proved to be the best in the market and made all the hard work worthwhile. From then on, Amarprakash Developers offers the best products and services with a high value for money to all its customers.
